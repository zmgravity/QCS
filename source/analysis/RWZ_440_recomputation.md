# RWZ recomputation of the normalized 220 × 220 → 440 coefficient

The calculation uses (M=1) and

\[
\omega_{220}=0.37367168441804183579349200298
-0.08896231568893569828046092718i.
\]

## 1. Linear parent

The Regge–Wheeler parent is written

\[
\psi_{220}^{\rm RW}=A_1(u)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nu^n,
\qquad u=1-\frac{2}{r},
\]

and the (a_n) are generated by the three-term Leaver recurrence.  The
normalized Chandrasekhar transformation gives the even-parity Zerilli parent
needed in the public EEE source.

## 2. Quadratic Zerilli equation

For equal parents the regularized public source reduces to

\[
\mathfrak S=
\mathcal F_1\psi_1^2+2\mathcal F_2\psi_1\psi_1'
+\mathcal F_4(\psi_1')^2,
\]

because the antisymmetric 
\(\mathcal F_3(\psi_1'\psi_2-\psi_1\psi_2')\) term vanishes.
The specialized source gives, for example at (r=3),

\[
\mathcal F_1=-0.1468994408-0.1008818589i,
\]
\[
\mathcal F_2=-0.06506261720-0.001020480853i,
\qquad
\mathcal F_4=0.1946052855-0.07546144825i.
\]

After factoring the quadratic QNM boundary behavior,

\[
\Psi_{44}=A_1(u)^2\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_nu^n,
\]

the sourced Zerilli equation gives a linear recurrence for (c_n).  The free
horizon coefficient (c_0) is selected by cancelling the nonminimal tail at
large (n).  The generic cross-source normalization used by
`QuadraticRatio.csv` must be retained here; the identical-parent factor
(1/2) is applied only when converting the normalized result to the physical
self-coupling ratio.

The stable (N=60\text{--}80) result is

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm RWZ}=0.57321+0.03740i.
\]

## 3. RWZ-to-TT reconstruction

Specializing the public `REEE` asymptotic relation gives the affine map

\[
\widehat{\mathcal R}_{EEE}
=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}\mathcal R_{\rm RWZ}
+0.01241195804-0.12180155013i.
\]

Using the (N=80), 12-tail result,

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm RWZ}=0.5732102338+0.0374028010i,
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{
\widehat{\mathcal R}_{EEE}
=0.9187369165-0.0626625292i
}
\]

which rounds to

\[
\boxed{0.919-0.0627i}.
\]

The relative complex discrepancy from the rounded tabulated number is
(2.9\times10^{-4}).

For (m=m_1=m_2=(4,2,2)),

\[
\begin{pmatrix}4&2&2\\-4&2&2\end{pmatrix}=\frac13,
\]

and the identical-parent symmetry factor is (2).  Therefore the physical
strain ratio is

\[
\mathcal R_{44(220)^2}
=\frac{1}{6}\widehat{\mathcal R}_{EEE}
=0.1531228194-0.0104437549i
=0.1534786e^{-0.0680996i},
\]

consistent with the published rounded value
(0.1535227e^{-0.0681208i}).
